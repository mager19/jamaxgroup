# Jamax Group site — design

Date: 2026-11-16 · Status: approved by owner in chat · Folder: `group/` · Domain: `jamaxgroup.com` (www redirects)

## Intent

A showcase for "anyone who wants to see what we do at Jamax" (owner). A visitor understands in seconds what Jamax Group is and jumps to the business they care about.

## Goals

- Present the three businesses, each with one line, its audience, a few tags and a link to its own site.
- Contact: WhatsApp 3001248065 and groupjamax@gmail.com.

## Non-goals and anti-goals

- No company history, team, clients, counts, years or awards. The owner is still defining these: "por ahora dejémoslo así mientras definimos".
- No claims beyond what the three sites already state.
- No form; contact is direct.

## Businesses (facts from the existing sites)

| Business | URL | Line | For | Facts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| JamaxTravel | https://travel.jamaxgroup.com | National and international travel plans, quoted over WhatsApp | Travelers | Promotions with dates, operated with a licensed wholesale operator |
| FotoSnap | https://fotosnap.jamaxgroup.com | Photo-printing kiosk: guests print from their own phone | Events and shopping malls | Pay per photo, all-inclusive event package, or mall space |
| Jamax Vending | https://vending.jamaxgroup.com | Snack and cold-drink vending machines installed at no cost | Residential complexes and offices | The complex earns 5 % of profit; service 24/7 |

Coverage shared by the businesses: Medellín metropolitan area (FotoSnap, Vending). Travel sells destinations, so coverage is not claimed for it.

## Approaches

1. **Astro static site in `group/`, same pattern as `kiosko/` and `vending/` (chosen).**
2. A plain HTML page: simpler, but breaks the shared tooling and test pattern.
3. Merging the three sites under one domain: out of scope; the owner wants each business on its own subdomain.

## Visual direction (reference: land-book "Stoa")

- **Palette and grid:** a warm off-white background, one orange accent and near-black text, with fine rules and a faint vertical column grid behind the content.
- **Headlines:** large, sentence case, medium weight and tight; the second sentence is in orange.
- **Labels:** small monospace labels such as `01 / LO QUE HACEMOS`. The reference pins these, which overrides the generic no-eyebrow rule.
- **Cards:** three service cards on a slightly darker surface, each with a small accent mark in that business's own color:
  - travel: its blue;
  - FotoSnap: coral;
  - Vending: lime.
- **Hero object:** a 3D orange sculptural object, generated by the owner in Nano Banana. Until it arrives, the hero is typographic with an authored SVG folded-ribbon mark.

## Structure

1. **Header:** the Jamax Group logo, anchors and a contact link.
2. **Hero:** "Negocios que operamos de principio a fin", a short lead and a link to the businesses.
3. **`#negocios` (01 / Lo que hacemos):** three cards linking to the sites.
4. **`#como-trabajamos` (02 / Cómo trabajamos):** three principles that are true across the sites:
   - "Operamos nosotros": we run the service ourselves (FotoSnap and Vending operate their machines; Travel quotes directly).
   - "Atención directa": answered by WhatsApp and email.
   - "Claro desde el inicio": each site states its conditions up front.
5. **`#contacto` (03 / Contacto):** WhatsApp and email.
6. **Footer:** the operator name and links to the three sites.

## Constraints

- Astro 7.3.8 and sharp 0.35.5, Node 22.20; the same `site.ts`/`waLink` pattern, with the env override `GROUP_WHATSAPP`.
- Spanish copy, neutral, addressing the reader as "tú" to match the consumer-facing brands.
- Tests:
  - the three outbound links exist with the exact URLs;
  - no digits-plus-"años", "clientes" counts, or percentages other than the vending 5 %;
  - the real contact appears;
  - adjacent sections differ in background;
  - headline words are at most 14 characters.
- Deploy:
  - a Vercel project with Root Directory `group`, domains `jamaxgroup.com` and `www.jamaxgroup.com`;
  - in Cloudflare, the apex record changes from the current Hostinger IP to Vercel (DNS only).
