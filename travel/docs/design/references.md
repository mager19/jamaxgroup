# Design References

Visual style (colors, type, spacing, components) is defined in `style-guide.md`.
The references below define **composition and layout only** — their colors are not used.

## 1. Home — "Trippple" (composition only)
- Centered hero: small pill badge above, two-line display headline mixing a sans with an accent phrase, short subcopy, single CTA.
- Logo/trust strip below the hero.
- Horizontal strip of travel photos in rounded cards with staggered heights.

## 2. Plan cards — "Aventra Travel" listing
- Photo on top with chips overlaid (e.g. duration).
- Title, origin → destination line with a pin icon.
- Price block at the bottom.
- **Adaptation for this project:** no fixed price and no payment. Show "Desde $X COP por persona" (reference price), "Salida sugerida" / season, and a WhatsApp quote CTA.

## 3. Trip detail — "Aventra Travel" detail
- Mosaic gallery (large center image + side tiles).
- Title, intro description, row of key facts (duration, accommodation, etc.).
- Trip overview, "What's included", day-by-day itinerary.
- Sticky side card with key info and CTA.
- Recommended trips row at the bottom.
- **Adaptation for this project:** the side-card CTA is "Cotizar gratis por WhatsApp" instead of "Book Now"; price shown as "Desde"; dates shown as suggested departure / season.
